ABOUT US

Welcome to Plantellect—your digital community companion for exploring and viewing plants identified through their leaves and stems, empowering you to explore the flora within your community.

WHAT WE DO

Plantellect is a dedicated community platform where users can explore and view plants uploaded by individuals who hold authority within the system. By focusing on key vegetative traits like stems and leaves, our platform connects you with plant entries to help you discover the flora right around you.

OUR MISSION

We aim to make botany accessible, educational, and engaging for students, gardeners, and nature enthusiasts alike. By centering around a collaborative community and focusing on physical traits like stems and leaves, Plantellect empowers users to explore the flora within their community.

COMMUNITY AND CONTRIBUTIONS

Our library is powered by contributions from individuals who have been granted authority to upload within the system. Users can easily browse these uploaded profiles to discover species names, classifications, and detailed descriptions of local plant life.
