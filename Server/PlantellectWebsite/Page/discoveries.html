ALUGBATI

Alugbati is a succulent, fast-growing vine that is common throughout the Philippines. It is easily recognized by its fleshy, heart-shaped, smooth leaves.

ARATILES

Aratiles is a fast-growing, evergreen tree featuring spreading branches, sticky leaves, and small white flowers.

MALUNGGAY

Malunggay is widely cultivated for its nutritious leaf vegetables, flower pods, and seeds, and is frequently used as a vegetable in soups.

SAMPAGUITA

Sampaguita is a species of jasmine native to tropical Asia, prized for its strongly scented small white flowers that bloom all year round.

GUMAMELA

Gumamela is a tropical shrub with large, prominent flowers that come in vibrant shades of red, pink, yellow, and orange.
